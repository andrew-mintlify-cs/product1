---
title: "Data extraction engine"
description: "Extract aggregated data from Product1 cubes for downstream systems."
---

The data extraction engine exports aggregated results from cubes to files or downstream systems on a schedule or on demand.

## Capabilities

- Export query results to Parquet or CSV.
- Schedule recurring extractions with cron expressions.
- Stream incremental extracts to object storage.
